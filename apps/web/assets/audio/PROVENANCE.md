# Audio provenance

The original files were generated with **ElevenLabs** using the project's key on a
**paid ElevenLabs plan** (confirmed by the project owner), then post-processed locally with ffmpeg (libopus).
No generation tooling, prompt files or intermediates are kept in the repository; the prompts are recorded below.

| Kind | Provider | API | Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Music | ElevenLabs Eleven Music | `POST /v1/music` (`force_instrumental: true`) | `music_v1` |
| Home I, Home II | ElevenLabs Eleven Music (structured sections) | `POST /v1/music` (`composition_plan`, seed) | `music_v2_5` |
| Sound effects | ElevenLabs Sound Effects | `POST /v1/sound-generation` | `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` |
| Lobby tracks, newer beds (Orbit 4–7, Sky 4–7, Surface 4–7, Depths 4–7) and battle tracks, stinger takes | Suno via Kie.ai | `POST https://api.kie.ai/api/v1/generate` (`customMode`, `instrumental: true`) | Suno `V5` |

The game has no voices (no announcer or speech): important moments use the non-verbal alert chirp and the
victory/defeat stingers.

Prompts are original descriptions of mood, instrumentation and tempo. They name no artist, composer, song, album,
label or game soundtrack (Eleven Music's input rules forbid that, and the brief asks for original work only).

## Processing

- **Music loops**: the steady section is detected (1 s RMS windows). The loop end is chosen within the last 6 s,
  where the energy envelope best matches the rhythm at the loop start, then refined to ±10 ms by waveform
  correlation. The loop head gets an equal-power crossfade (6 s, 4 s for the battle tracks) with the material after the
  loop end, so the end flows into the start. Loudness: two-pass EBU R128 `loudnorm` (linear) to −16 LUFS (home,
  stingers) or −18 LUFS (plane beds and battle tracks), true peak −1.5 dBTP. Opus 64 kb/s stereo.
- **Home I and Home II (replacing the previous home theme, which was deleted)**: candidates c and d of four
  Eleven Music v2.5 generations of one ten-section composition plan for a heavy, reverb-drenched electric-guitar metal
  anthem. The sections are intro build, main riff, lead melody, main riff again, development, breakdown, guitar solo,
  build, return and an outro back to the intro. The user listened to the candidates and picked c and d; a and b were
  not used. Nothing was edited musically.
  - **Loops:** each loop starts at the first audible note and ends a whole number of four-bar phrases later (tempo
    measured from the audio: 121.85 and 139.55 BPM). The end is the phrase boundary in the last ~60 s whose
    energy and level best match the opening, refined to ±10 ms, with a two-bar equal-power crossfade. Home I loops
    196 bars (386.0 s) and Home II 232 bars (399.0 s).
  - **Loudness:** both are normalised to −16 LUFS like the previous menu music (sources -11.1 and
    -10.2 LUFS).
  - **Streaming:** each is cut into 30 s segment files (`home1_1`…`home1_13`,
    `home2_1`…`home2_13`), each with 2400 frames of its neighbours on both sides. The engine
    streams them back to back on the audio clock and heals each join (`healSegmentJoin`). Only `home2_1` (Home II, the
    default home theme) is in the initial download.
  - **Playback:** at home Auto starts on Home II (the default home theme), then Home I, then Home II again. Each hands over to the next 3 s before
    the end of its pass with a crossfade, and the next track's first file is decoded ahead.
- **Suno tracks**: instrumental mode, chosen by the project owner after listening to every candidate (all were
  kept). No musical edits. Each loop starts at the first audible note and ends a whole number of four-bar phrases
  later. Tempo is measured from the audio. The end is the phrase boundary in the last ~60 s before any fade-out whose
  energy and level best match the opening, refined to ±10 ms, with a two-bar equal-power crossfade. Loudness is
  −18 LUFS for battle tracks and beds and −16 LUFS for the lobby tracks and stingers (menu level). Opus 64 kb/s
  stereo; loops stream in 30 s segment files. Stinger takes are trimmed of silence with a 0.4 s tail fade.
- **Suno plane beds (Sky 4–7, Surface 4–7, Depths 6–7)**: instrumental mode, picked by analysis from 24 takes (12
  prompts; the project owner did not listen first). Picks favour the best loop match and a steady level. Every pick
  passed Suno's 2-stem separation (Kie.ai `ai-music-api/separate-vocals`): its vocal stem stays at least 89 dB under
  the mix across the shipped loop. Takes with vocal-stem activity inside the loop were dropped, and so were the two
  "Magma Vein" takes, which Kie.ai refused to separate as matching "an existing recording in our catalog". No musical
  edits. Each loop starts at the first note or a later four-bar phrase in the opening minute (past a quiet intro) and
  ends a whole number of four-bar phrases later in the last ~75 s before any fade-out, where the next four bars best
  match the loop's opening (band rhythm pattern, spectral shape, level); searched ±1 beat, refined ±10 ms on the
  waveform, with a two-bar equal-power crossfade into the opening. Loudness −18 LUFS by linear gain (no limiting).
  Opus 64 kb/s stereo in 30 s segment files.
- **Loop files** (music and ambience) carry 2400 frames (50 ms) of wrap padding at each end. The playable loop is
  [2400, 2400 + loop frames) at 48 kHz (`LOOP_FRAMES` in `src/audio/catalog.ts`). The engine also crossfades
  the last 1024 decoded frames into the pre-roll (`healLoopSeam`), so lossy decoding cannot click at the seam.
- **One-shot effects**: silence is trimmed (−48 dB), with 2 ms and 30 ms edge fades. Gain is set from the loudest
  300 ms window (−14 dBFS RMS, −20 for UI), peak ≤ −1 dBFS. Mono Opus 48 kb/s. Ambience and activity loops get a 1 s
  crossfade (−20 dBFS RMS): stereo 64 kb/s for wind/storm, mono 48 kb/s for engine, drill, tread, tow, machinery,
  rotor, flag, gate, radiation and yard loops.

Total: 124 sounds (219 files), 39012.9 KiB. Initial download after the home is interactive: `music/home2_1` (Home II,
the default home theme) + three UI clicks = 229.5 KiB. The rest of the home themes streams while they play; everything
else loads on demand in matches.

## SFX mix and Orbit mining

Six shipped files below were generated through the existing paid **ElevenLabs Sound Effects** endpoint
`POST https://api.elevenlabs.io/v1/sound-generation?output_format=mp3_44100_128`, model
`eleven_text_to_sound_v2`. Five are new; `construction` replaces the original. No music was generated or edited.
The synchronous responses did not expose a request id in `request-id` or `x-request-id`; output SHA-256 hashes
identify the shipped takes below. Original prompts contain no artist/game soundtrack references or speech.
Two weak, transient-dominated first takes of depletion/release were discarded, not shipped.

Processing follows the existing 48 kHz mono / Opus 48 kb/s SFX pipeline: −48 dB trim, 2 ms attack / 30 ms tail
fades, loudest 300 ms RMS target −14 dBFS with a conservative **−2 dBFS pre-encode peak** to allow codec overshoot.
Peak-limited cues are not forced to the RMS target. The mining loop uses a four-second playable span, one-second
equal-power crossfade, −20 dBFS overall RMS, 2400-frame circular padding at each end, and the existing runtime
1024-frame seam repair. Its encoded duration includes the 0.1 s padding. Helpers and intermediate sources remain
outside the repository; no credentials are distributed.

Mix decisions: repeated weapons reduced by approximately 0.7–2 dB, small impacts by 3 dB, large explosions by
approximately 1.5 dB, and rapid fire gaps modestly lengthened. The high-crest UI select tick drops 3.1 dB while
confirm/error stay unchanged. Rotor, gate, flag and radiation loops drop 1.9–3.1 dB rather than competing with
utility cues. Construction's replacement and gain 0.75 lift its loudest 300 ms at the SFX bus from −24.8 to
−20.1 dBFS (research −19.2); alerts retain 0.7 and get their own one-voice category, independent of economy receipts.
A final −3 dB threshold, 20:1, zero-knee compressor (2 ms attack / 120 ms release) catches dense summed transients;
it is a safety compressor, not a guaranteed brick-wall limiter. Master/music/SFX settings and mute remain unchanged.

Routing: Orbit mining starts (`mining_start`) sound `mine_extract` once, continuous visible mining Tugs feed the
two-voice `mine_loop` activity pool, `mining_depleted` sounds exhaustion, and own `mining_unload` sounds the receipt.
These are non-public FX subject to the existing own-or-visible fog gate and first-view/deduplication rules;
delivery receipts additionally require our army even when an enemy delivery is visible.
Deliberate `deorbit` release sounds `tug_drop`, distinct from the grapple-lock `tug_tow`; no damage rules change.
All five new sounds are Orbit-only. Existing Surface assets now play for flamer shots, active Drill Yards, and
own `produced` / `breacher` conversion events. Activity still obeys camera distance, pan, zoom and plane gates.

The processing and playback measurements are technical evidence, **not a subjective listening approval**.
