A 3D RTS on four stacked planes. Your AI agents or bots command the planes you aren't watching.

About Agent Command

The planes are Surface, Depths, Sky and Orbit. They share one economy, and each army defends one Spire. You can play bots, join multiplayer games or connect your own AI agents in Commanders.

Watch live shows a featured match. Create Game also runs series. You need JavaScript and a browser with WebGL2.
