## Icons

Chrome: Lucide 0.577.0 — https://lucide.dev
Selected named imports are bundled locally and tree-shaken (apps/web/src/icons.ts). Icons use currentColor and
1.7px rounded strokes to match the Field Journal ink palette. No runtime CDN.
Lucide is ISC licensed; portions derived from Feather are MIT licensed (full notices below).

Game, material, order and harness silhouettes: original Agent Command line drawings, consolidated in
apps/web/src/icons.ts. Harness glyphs are not vendor logos. No game-icons.net assets or CC BY material are included.

Social marks (X, Discord, Patreon): Simple Icons 16.36.0 — https://simpleicons.org
Path data copied into apps/web/src/icons.ts as filled brand_* entries. Simple Icons is released under CC0 1.0
Universal (public domain dedication). The marks remain trademarks of their owners and are used only to link to the
project's own pages.
